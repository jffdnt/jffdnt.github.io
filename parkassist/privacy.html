Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026

This policy describes how ParkAssist Copilot handles information when an authorized user accesses the service.

Information processed

The service processes the signed-in user's Microsoft Entra authentication token and live garage data needed to answer a request. Garage data can include space status, floor and designation, sensor and camera health, camera thumbnails, and license-plate data for occupied-space searches.

Purpose and access

Information is used only to provide garage operations and parking-assistance features. Access is limited to users authorized by the publisher's Microsoft Entra tenant. The service does not sell personal information or use garage data for advertising.

Storage and logs

The ParkAssist Copilot service does not create a separate persistent store of garage records, camera images, or plate-search queries. Operational logs record request timing, route, status, and a correlation identifier; request bodies and query strings are excluded. Microsoft 365, Microsoft Azure, and upstream garage systems may retain information according to their administrators' policies.

Security

The service uses tenant authentication, encrypted HTTPS connections, short-lived signed image links, least-privilege application access, and data-minimized responses. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

Do not use the service for a plate lookup unless you are authorized to do so. Contact your tenant administrator to request access changes or ask about organizational retention requirements.
