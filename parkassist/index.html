ParkAssist Copilot

Publisher and support information

ParkAssist Copilot is a read-only assistant for authorized users of the 5 Bell parking garage. It provides live garage status, available-space lookup, occupied-space plate lookup, and camera-feed health information through Microsoft 365 Copilot and Copilot Studio.

Access is restricted through the publisher's Microsoft Entra tenant. This site contains public policy and support information; it does not provide access to garage data.
